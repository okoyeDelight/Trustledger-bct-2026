381 of 387 gaps ended with a normal reading.

Across the organizer's full synthetic year, the next recorded value after 381 monitoring gaps was within the illustrative 2–6°C band. In 375 cases, both readings bordering the missing interval were in range. Those normal endpoints do not tell us what happened in the missing hours.

This is descriptive evidence of a latest-value blind spot. A competent gap-aware rules monitor would detect these interruptions too; we have not demonstrated superior prediction or patient outcomes.

SOURCE GENERATOR EXPLAINS THE GAP

No timestamp ≠ no delivery

The synthetic generator computes arrival, then randomly removes the receipt field in about 3% of transfers. It independently assigns the unit to its destination.

What we can conclude: a receiving record is incomplete and merits reconciliation.

What we cannot conclude: physical non-delivery, an unsafe blood unit, a clinical hold requirement, or patient harm.

Decision: Keep this as a data-quality example, but not our main clinical-safety case.

All values are fictional organizer-generated records. The generator's internal arrival calculation would not be available as evidence to real hospital staff. No independent clinical decision is derived from this source field. Reproduce this audit ↗

WHY LATEST-ONLY MISSES IT

Normal ≠ verified.

For HSP-024, 3.9°C was followed 58 hours later by 4.3°C. There are 57 missing expected hourly records. The system cannot infer what happened during the gap.

THE HONEST LIMIT

Rules may be enough.

A competent conventional monitor that checks gaps, outliers and excursions catches the same four cases. We do not claim an AI advantage or clinical benefit from this comparison.

01 / SOURCE

Verified synthetic temperature rows

Case readings are copied from the organizers' supplied temperature_logs.csv. HSP-024's gap, RBTC-NBO's documented 7.9°C reading and the 99.9°C sentinel can be cross-checked in that file. All timestamps use East Africa Time (UTC+3).

02 / THE MISSING LINK

Unit assignments are added fixtures

The starter temperature feed is facility-level, not named-fridge telemetry. DEMO-* identifiers and FR-A/B/C/D equipment associations are added synthetic examples. These associations do not establish any real unit's exposure or actual disposition.

03 / HUMAN CONTROL

No autonomous clinical release

An operator may propose an action. A simulated quality reviewer must Accept, Change or Reject it. Mock issues stay blocked for unresolved evidence. This browser demo simulates roles; it does not provide production authentication or lab authorization.

04 / FALLBACK

Auditable deterministic checks

The inspection rules run in the browser with no AI endpoint. A service worker caches the application after the first successful visit; local decisions persist in this browser. This is a demonstrable offline-capable prototype, not synchronized multi-device storage.

Site-specific policy required: The demo's 2–6°C RBC comparison and −10 to 25°C sanity band are illustrative engineering settings, not transfusion SOPs. This interface does not assess platelets, plasma, compatibility, viability or patient priority.
